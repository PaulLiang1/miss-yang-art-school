Meet Miss Yang认识杨老师

I believe every child has a unique artistic voice. For younger children, I focus on nurturing imagination, curiosity and creative confidence.我相信每个孩子都有独特的艺术语言。对于年幼的孩子，我注重呵护他们的想象力、好奇心和创作自信。

As students grow, I provide personalised guidance and structured art education, helping them develop strong technical skills while discovering their own artistic style.随着孩子成长，我会提供个性化指导和系统的美术教育，帮助他们打下扎实的绘画功底，同时找到属于自己的艺术风格。

Nurturing imagination. Building skills. Inspiring individuality.呵护想象，夯实基础，激发个性。

How we teach我们的教育理念

Guiding young creativity with expertise, nurturing growth through art.用专业守护童心，用艺术陪伴成长。

  1. 01

    Observation and understanding观察与理解能力

    Children learn to look properly before they draw: at shape, light, colour and the way a thing is put together.先学会看，再动手画。孩子观察形状、光线、颜色，理解事物是怎么组合在一起的。

  2. 02

    Creative thinking创意思维能力

    Ideas are welcomed, then tested. Students are encouraged to imagine, experiment and make choices of their own.鼓励大胆想象，也鼓励把想法画出来、试一试，逐渐形成自己的选择。

  3. 03

    Making and creating independently独立创作能力

    With guidance that steps back over time, students take a work from first idea to finished piece on their own.老师的指导会随着孩子成长慢慢退后，让孩子独立完成从想法到作品的全过程。

Signature Art Projects特色艺术创作项目

Long-form projects students take from a first idea all the way to a finished piece.这些是学生从最初的想法一直做到完成作品的长线创作项目。

  • Four-panel comics四格漫画

    Students tell a small story in four beats: characters, setting, and one clear moment of change.把一个小故事拆成四个画面，安排角色与场景，再用图画讲清楚一个转折。

  • Original picture books原创绘本

    A whole book, from storyboard to cover, written and illustrated by the student.从故事板到封面，学生自己写、自己画，完成一本完整的绘本。

  • Character design角色设计

    Characters are built from shapes, expressions and costume, then drawn again in new poses.从形状、表情和服装入手设计角色，再让角色在新的动作里成立。

  • Scene design场景设计

    Students design spaces and mood: interiors, city corners, imagined worlds.设计空间与氛围：室内、街角，或者想象出来的世界。

Across the year: visual storytelling and drawing from life.全年贯穿：视觉叙事艺术与观察写生。

  • Parent and child art亲子艺术体验 An art experience for a child and a grown-up to share.家长与孩子共同参与的艺术体验。
