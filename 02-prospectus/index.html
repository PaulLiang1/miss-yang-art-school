02

How we teach教学方式

Three abilities, built together. Every lesson is planned around them.三种能力，同步培养。每一节课都围绕它们展开。

Observe观察

Observation and understanding观察与理解

Visual observation, analytical thinking and spatial awareness, and an understanding of shape, structure, light and colour.培养观察力、分析思维与空间认知，理解造型、结构、光影与色彩。

Think思考

Creative thinking创意思维

Imagination, curiosity, original ideas, exploration and creative problem-solving.保护想象力与好奇心，发展原创想法、探索精神与解决问题的能力。

Create创作

Independent creation独立创作

Planning, artistic decisions, personal expression and the ability to complete original artworks independently.自主构思、艺术判断、个人表达，并独立完成原创作品。

Meet Miss Yang认识杨老师

Artist, educator, founder.艺术家、美术教育者、创始人。

Alina Yang, founder, artist and art educatorAlina Yang，创始人、艺术家、美术教育者

I believe every child has a unique artistic voice. With younger children I focus on nurturing imagination, curiosity and creative confidence.我相信每个孩子都有独特的艺术语言。对于年幼的孩子，我注重呵护他们的想象力、好奇心和创作自信。

As students grow, I give them personalised guidance and a structured art education, so they build strong technical skills and find their own artistic style.随着孩子成长，我会提供个性化指导和系统的美术教育，帮助他们打下扎实功底，同时找到属于自己的艺术风格。

Nurturing imagination. Building skills. Inspiring individuality.呵护想象，夯实基础，激发个性。
